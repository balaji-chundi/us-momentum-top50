How to read this list

Momentum score

The stock's return over roughly the last 6 months, leaving out the most recent month. Higher means a stronger, steadier uptrend. The bar compares it with the #1 stock.

1D · 1W · 1M

Price change over the last 1, 5 and 21 trading days, from closing prices. ▲ green is up, ▼ red is down. A strong trend with a red week is a pullback, not necessarily a reversal.

Held

The us_momentum paper strategy holds the top 10 names and re-balances every 21 trading days, so a stock can sit in the top 10 here without being bought yet.

Stale

The stock's latest price is older than everyone else's, usually a trading halt or a late data update. Treat its numbers with caution until the next refresh.
